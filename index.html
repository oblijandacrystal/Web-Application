Our Project: Book Researcher App

The Book Researcher is a search tool built for students and researchers. A user types a title, author, or keyword and gets back a list of matching books.

Explore Our App →

Each result shows:

  • Cover image For quick visual recognition of edition.
  • author(s) To identify who wrote the work.
  • publication year To show whether a book is recent or older

Main Goal

Its main purpose is to help academics quickly find the relevant books without getting unrelated results. It helps then explore a topic, find a specific book, and identify the write about the subject.
